Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 27-11-2026 Last Updated: 27-11-2026

Urbaniac Studio ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to handling player information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how Urbaniac collects, uses, stores, retains, and protects information when you interact with our Discord application.

By using Urbaniac, you acknowledge that your information may be processed as described in this Privacy Policy.

1. About This Policy

Urbaniac is an urban life simulation RPG operating through Discord.

This Privacy Policy applies to information processed by Urbaniac in connection with its gameplay features and services.

Urbaniac Studio is responsible for the operation of the application and the personal data processing described in this Policy.

Application: Urbaniac

Operator: Urbaniac Studio

Database: PostgreSQL

Hosting Environment: Locally hosted / self-managed infrastructure.

2. Information We Collect

We collect and process information necessary to operate gameplay features and maintain player progression.

2.1 Discord Account Information

We process:

  • Discord User ID.
  • Discord username.

These identifiers allow us to associate gameplay information with the correct player.

We do not request your Discord password.

2.2 Gameplay Information

When you interact with Urbaniac, we may store:

  • Player level.
  • Experience points (XP).
  • Inventory information.
  • In-game items.
  • Virtual currency balances.
  • Virtual currency transaction records.
  • Activity and gameplay history.

This information is necessary to maintain player progression, gameplay state, and game functionality.

2.3 Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Based on the current data collection scope, Urbaniac does not intentionally request:

  • Discord passwords.
  • Personal email addresses.
  • Telephone numbers.
  • Government identification numbers.
  • Payment card information.
  • Precise real-world location information.

If future features require additional personal information, this Policy will be updated before those features are introduced.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

3.1 Account Identification

To identify players and associate their Discord accounts with their game profiles.

3.2 Gameplay Operations

To operate and maintain:

  • Character progression.
  • XP and leveling systems.
  • Inventory and item management.
  • Resource gathering.
  • Crafting and production.
  • Virtual currency and transactions.
  • Activities and gameplay history.
  • City progression.

3.3 Game Integrity

To detect suspicious gameplay behavior, investigate exploits, prevent unauthorized manipulation, and maintain fair gameplay.

3.4 Service Maintenance

To troubleshoot technical problems, maintain application functionality, and improve gameplay systems.

4. Database and Data Storage

Urbaniac currently uses PostgreSQL as its primary database.

The application operates in a locally hosted, self-managed environment.

Gameplay information is stored in the database to support persistent player profiles and gameplay functionality.

We do not claim that data is hosted on a third-party cloud platform.

The physical hosting location, backup configuration, and specific infrastructure security controls depend on the actual deployment environment.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell player personal information.

Information may be processed or disclosed in the following circumstances:

5.1 Discord

Urbaniac operates through Discord's platform and APIs.

Discord independently processes information under its own Privacy Policy and applicable terms.

Your interaction with Urbaniac may therefore involve data processing by Discord.

Discord Privacy Policy: https://discord.com/privacy

5.2 Service Providers

If external service providers are used to support the operation of Urbaniac, they may process information only as necessary to provide their services and subject to appropriate contractual and legal safeguards.

Any such providers and their relevant processing activities will be reflected in this Policy when applicable.

5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by applicable laws, valid legal processes, or competent authorities.

6. Data Retention

We retain player information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate Urbaniac and fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Gameplay records may be retained while a player account remains active.

When information is no longer necessary, or when a valid deletion request is received, we will take appropriate steps to delete or anonymize the relevant information, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

Where backups exist, deletion from backup copies may require additional time according to the actual backup and recovery process.

We do not promise a specific deletion period until an operational retention schedule has been established.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures intended to protect player information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, and destruction.

Our security practices may include access restrictions and appropriate database and infrastructure safeguards.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should also protect their Discord accounts and avoid sharing account credentials.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Request information about how your data is processed.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Exercise other rights available under applicable data protection laws.

For users protected by Indonesian Law No. 27 of 2022 concerning Personal Data Protection, applicable rights under that legislation are recognized.

9. Data Access and Deletion Requests

You may submit a request regarding your personal information by contacting Urbaniac Studio.

Please include:

  • Your Discord User ID.
  • A description of your request.
  • Sufficient information to verify that you control the relevant account.

We may request reasonable additional information to verify your identity before processing a request.

We will review and respond to valid requests within the timeframes required by applicable law.

Where deletion is legally required and no valid retention obligation applies, we will take appropriate steps to delete the relevant information.

Contact: Urbaniac Studio

Email: urbaniac.support@gmail.com

Support Server: https://discord.gg/sRUcs3NZpC

10. International Data Processing

Although Urbaniac uses a locally hosted application environment, Discord operates independently and may process information through infrastructure located in other jurisdictions.

Where personal information is transferred internationally by Urbaniac or its service providers, applicable legal requirements and safeguards will apply.

11. Children's Privacy

Urbaniac is provided through Discord and is subject to Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable local laws.

We do not intentionally collect personal information directly from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe information has been collected unlawfully, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our gameplay features, infrastructure, data processing practices, or legal obligations change.

Updated versions will be published at the official Privacy Policy URL.

The latest revision date will appear at the beginning of this document.
