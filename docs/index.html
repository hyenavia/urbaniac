Urbaniac gameplay systems

Built around persistent progression, transparent ledgers, and zero-debt city management.

Activities · Crew

Offline activities and crew

Run Farming, Gathering, Fishing, and Mining across 2 offline slots. Hire 4 crew members for Yield, Luck, Efficiency, and AUTO restarts.

Economy · Market

Dynamic NPC market

Personal Wallet and City Treasury stay separate with idempotent ledgers. Place up to 5 Broker listings at +10%, +25%, or +40% prices.

Crafting · Orders

Tiered crafting and orders

Refine Raw goods into Processed and Finished items across 2 crafting slots. Fulfill up to 3 NPC Orders for a +25% bonus payout.

City · Treasury

Zero-debt city governance

Citizen Tax (5%, 10%, or 15%) funds maintenance and crew salaries in your City Treasury. Balances never go negative or accrue fines.
