Core gameplay systems

Built around persistent progression, transparent ledgers, and zero-debt city management.

Activities · Crew

Offline activities and crew automation

Farming, Gathering, Fishing, and Mining run across 2 active slots even while you are offline, with DM alerts when rewards are ready. Recruit up to 4 crew members (100 Bac each) for Efficiency, Yield, Luck, and AUTO restarts.

Economy · Market

Dynamic NPC market and broker listings

Personal Wallet and City Treasury stay strictly separate with idempotent ledgers. NPC buy prices shift with Global Seasons and World Events, and you can place up to 5 Broker listings at +10%, +25%, or +40% target prices without P2P trading exploits.

Crafting · Orders

Tiered crafting and NPC orders

Process resources from Raw to Processed to Finished across 2 parallel crafting slots with optional Auto-Sell. Fulfill up to 3 active NPC Orders for a +25% base payout bonus plus +3% per Shop Reputation level.

City · Treasury

Tax, welfare, and zero-debt city governance

Automatic Citizen Tax (5%, 10%, or 15%) funds facility maintenance and crew salaries in your City Treasury. Balances never go negative and carry no fines; an empty treasury lowers Welfare and activity effectiveness gradually.

Played directly in Discord

Press Play in the server to open your own Private Thread. Interactive menus are locked to their owner, and persistent schedulers send DM notifications whenever tasks finish.
